description: Interview practice on what we built so far
---
Look at the code built so far and act as the IncuBrix live-session interviewer.
Ask me ONE question at a time (architecture walkthrough, predict-the-output, a debugging
scenario, or a small live change). Wait for my answer, then score it 0/1/3/5 like the
brief, tell me the ideal answer, and ask the next. 5 questions total. Do not change code.
