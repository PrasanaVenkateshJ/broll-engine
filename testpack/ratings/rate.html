Rate the B-roll

How to rate. Play each scene (only that part of the video plays) and ask: does the picture fit what the narration means at that moment?
  • 5 = clearly matches the meaning  ·  4 = good match
  • 3 = acceptable (related, a viewer would not mind)
  • 2 = weak (only loosely related)  ·  1 = wrong or nonsense

Rate the footage, not the captions or the voice. A generated number card counts as a match if it shows the number the line says. Progress is saved in this browser; use Export CSV and save the file as testpack/ratings/ratings.csv, then run tools/evaluate.py.
